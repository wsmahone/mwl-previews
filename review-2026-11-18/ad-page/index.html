Who this program is for

We don't sell one weight-loss program. We determine what may be preventing you from losing weight and build the treatment around you.

One person may struggle primarily with hunger. Another may be a night eater or frequent snacker. Someone else may have difficulty planning meals, may need more structure, or may have medical conditions that affect their weight.

Whether you want to lose 30 pounds, 50 pounds, 100 pounds or more, our goal is not simply to help you lose weight. We want to help you improve your health and develop a realistic strategy for maintaining your progress long term.

Our team works with patients throughout Syracuse and Central New York who have struggled with their weight, often after trying multiple diets, exercise programs, or weight-loss plans without lasting success.

How the Program Works

We start with a blank slate. It begins with a free Discovery Call.

  1. Schedule your free Discovery Call

    A free call with our team. We go over how the program works, answer your questions, and review pricing. Nothing medical is decided on this call.

  2. Complete your evaluation

    Your first office visit is with a nurse for testing and bloodwork, then a visit of about 60 minutes with a provider and about 45 minutes with our registered dietitian. Your plan is designed from your individual results after a complete physical and a nutrition visit, not before them.

  3. Your plan and follow-up

    Your plan is designed from your individual results, with treatment options reviewed at your visit and considered case by case when it is clinically appropriate. Regular follow-up visits monitor your progress, identify problems early, and adjust your plan along the way. When you reach your goal, the focus changes from losing weight to maintaining it through our weight maintenance program.

What your first visits include

  1. Complete physical, Part 1

    Your first office visit is with a nurse for testing and bloodwork: resting metabolic rate (RMR) testing, Tanita body-composition analysis including muscle mass, blood pressure and pulse ox, and an in-office blood draw. Recent bloodwork from your own doctor can be reviewed. An EKG is not part of this visit.

  2. Complete physical, Part 2

    A visit of about 60 minutes with a provider, who reviews your bloodwork, medical history, eating patterns, and past experiences with you. If your provider decides an EKG is medically necessary, one is ordered, and it costs $95.

  3. Nutrition visit

    About 45 minutes with our registered dietitian to strategize your daily plan. Meal replacements are optional.

What the first visits cost. All three are one complete initial package, $249, with no long-term contract. You pay the $249 at your Part 1 visit. Pricing as of November 2026.

Will I have to follow a specific diet?

There is no perfect diet. The best nutritional plan is one that helps you lose weight, supports your health, and is realistic enough to follow in your everyday life.

You may have done well with a low-carbohydrate or keto-style plan in the past but no longer have the time to shop, cook, and plan every meal. In that situation, a more structured program or the use of meal replacements may make more sense. Other patients may prefer a food-based plan using meals they prepare at home.

What if my problem is snacking, night eating, or stress eating?

These behaviors are not all treated the same way.

Some people struggle with frequent snacking. Others eat most of their calories at night, experience episodes of overeating, eat in response to stress, or have difficulty controlling hunger in certain situations. We work to identify your individual eating patterns and develop strategies specifically for the problems that are getting in your way. Depending on the situation, treatment may involve behavioral strategies, nutritional changes, or a combination of approaches.

How much exercise will I need to do?

For some patients, suddenly increasing exercise can substantially increase hunger and make it harder to stay on their nutritional plan.

We help you develop an activity strategy based on your current fitness level, health, hunger response, and stage of weight loss. As you progress and move closer to weight maintenance, exercise can be gradually increased to help improve fitness, support lean mass, preserve your progress, and support long-term health.

Does my plan change as I lose weight?

A successful weight-loss program should not remain exactly the same from beginning to end.

Your hunger can change. Your nutritional needs can change. Your exercise capacity improves. Strategies that worked during the first few weeks of weight loss may not be the same strategies you need six months later. That is why ongoing follow-up is such an important part of our approach. We monitor your progress, identify problems early, and adjust your plan along the way to help you continue moving toward your goals.
