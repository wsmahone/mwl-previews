The requirement

What does Excellus ask for before a GLP-1 prior authorization?

Excellus asks for three consecutive months of participation in a Comprehensive Weight Management Program (CWMP), with four areas documented. It applies to members seeking authorization for certain GLP-1 medications.

Three months of participation

The current month and the two months immediately before it. Excellus's published policy asks for regularly scheduled sessions, at least monthly, with attendance on record.

Four areas, documented

  • Nutrition or diet
  • Exercise
  • Behavioral coaching
  • Progress tracking

What Excellus decides on its own

The program is one part of a prior authorization. Excellus also applies its own medical criteria and the benefits of your plan. For what your plan covers and requires, call Excellus member services.

The published policy cited on this page is Excellus drug policy PHARMACY-03 with its Guidelines for Comprehensive Weight Management Program, last reviewed August 24, 2026.

What Excellus confirmed about our program

  • Our program is an acceptable Comprehensive Weight Management Program.
  • Our patients need no other program to meet this requirement.
  • We can provide the documentation, and no specific format is required.
  • Current patients with sufficient documentation may be able to ask Excellus to look again at an authorization it denied before.

Confirmed in writing. As of November 2026.

What does our program do in each of the four areas?

All four are regular parts of our program. Your care is delivered by Dr. Scinta and her team of nurse practitioners and a registered dietitian, working from one chart in one office.

  1. Nutrition or diet

    A visit of about 45 minutes with our registered dietitian to strategize your daily plan, then personalized nutrition counseling and meal planning. Meal replacements are optional.

  2. Exercise

    Exercise guidance based on your current fitness level, health, hunger response and stage of weight loss. It is increased gradually as you progress.

  3. Behavioral coaching

    We work to identify your individual eating patterns, such as frequent snacking, night eating or stress eating, and develop strategies for the problems that are getting in your way.

  4. Progress tracking

    Tanita body-composition analysis, including muscle mass, and regular follow-up visits that monitor your progress, identify problems early and adjust your plan along the way.

Your first visits also include a complete physical: testing and bloodwork with a nurse and a visit of about 60 minutes with a provider. Your plan is designed from your individual results.

What do we document, and what do you get?

We can provide documentation of your participation in our program for your Excellus authorization. Excellus has confirmed that no specific format is required.

  • The months you took part in the program
  • Nutrition or diet
  • Exercise
  • Behavioral coaching
  • Progress tracking

Who does this help?

People with Excellus coverage whose plan asks for a Comprehensive Weight Management Program before it will consider a GLP-1 authorization.

  • New patients

    You can meet the program requirement here, and you need no other program. Your three months begin when you start taking part in the program.

  • Current patients who were denied before

    If an authorization was denied before and your record with us has sufficient documentation, you may be able to ask Excellus to look again. Excellus decides whether to look again, and what the answer is. Call us at 315-445-0023 and we will review your record with you.

What does this not mean?

It means our program counts for one Excellus requirement. The decision on a medication stays with Excellus.

  • It is not approval of a medication. Excellus decides each authorization from your benefits, your medical eligibility and your documented participation.
  • It does not make us in network. We are out of network, we do not bill insurance for visits, and we can provide a superbill.
  • It does not tell you what your plan covers. Whether a GLP-1 medication is covered depends on your plan and on the medical criteria Excellus sets. For those two questions, call Excellus member services.
  • It does not apply the same way to every Excellus plan. Excellus's published policy lists its commercial group, individual and Essential Plan coverage, and not its Medicare or Medicaid plans. Some employer plans do not cover these medications at all.
  • It does not mean a GLP-1 is right for you. A full medical evaluation makes that decision.
  • It does not apply to other insurers. This confirmation came from Excellus only.

Questions about the Excellus requirement

Are you in network with Excellus?

No. We are out of network with Excellus and do not bill insurance for visits.

We can provide a superbill, an itemized receipt you can send to your insurer to ask for reimbursement. Whether your plan pays any of it back is up to your plan. We accept HSA and FSA cards, CareCredit and Cherry payment plans.

Does this mean my medication will be approved?

No. Recognition of the program is not approval of a medication.

Excellus decides each authorization from your benefits, your medical eligibility and your documented participation. Whether a GLP-1 is prescribed at all is decided at your medical evaluation.

I was denied before. Can it be looked at again?

Possibly. Excellus has told us that current patients with sufficient documentation may be able to ask Excellus to look again at an authorization it denied before.

Excellus decides whether to look again, and what the answer is. Call us at 315-445-0023 and we will review your record with you.

How long before I have the three months?

Excellus asks for three consecutive months: the current month and the two months immediately before it.

Plan on at least three months in the program, with a session in each. How Excellus counts a month you joined partway through is a question for Excellus.

Do visits by themselves count?

Excellus asks for participation with four areas documented: nutrition or diet, exercise, behavioral coaching and progress tracking.

Excellus's published policy asks for regularly scheduled sessions, at least monthly, with attendance on record. Our follow-up visits are documented by date. Our program covers all four areas, and we document them. For how Excellus weighs your own record, ask Excellus.

What do I get from you to send to Excellus?

Documentation of your participation in our program.

It covers the months you took part and the four areas Excellus asks about. Excellus has confirmed that no specific format is required. Call the office when you need it.

Does this apply to other insurers?

No. This confirmation is from Excellus BlueCross BlueShield only.

Other insurers set their own rules. Ask your plan what it requires, and we can tell you what our program includes and documents.

Do I need another program as well?

No. Excellus has confirmed that patients in our program need no other program to meet its Comprehensive Weight Management Program requirement.

The rest of an authorization, including your benefits and your medical eligibility, is still decided by Excellus.
