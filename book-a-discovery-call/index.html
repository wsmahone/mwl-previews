Questions before you book

Is the Discovery Call really free?

Yes. The Discovery Call is free for our weight loss, GLP-1, BOUNCE, menopause, hormone therapy and testosterone programs. Peptide therapy, Jet Plasma and the GLP-1 Optimization Program start with a paid consultation instead, and the price is shown on the form when you choose one.

Is the Discovery Call a medical visit?

No. It is a phone call with our team about how the program works, what the first visit involves and what it costs. Nothing medical is decided on the call.

How long is the call?

Up to 30 minutes. We call you first to set a time that suits you.

Can someone join me on the call?

Yes. Anyone you choose is welcome, and parents or guardians join every step of the BOUNCE program for kids and teens.

Do you take insurance?

We are an out-of-network practice and do not bill insurance directly. We can provide a superbill for you to submit. Payment options include cash, check, major credit cards, HSA and FSA cards, CareCredit and Cherry payment plans.

What if I am not sure which program fits?

Pick the closest one on the form. We talk it through on the call. Weight and hormone care are handled by one team here, working from one chart, so you do not have to get the choice exactly right.
