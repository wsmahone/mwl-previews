  • Weight loss

    Every weight loss program starts with the same medical evaluation and a nutrition visit. Medication only when it is appropriate for you.

    All programs on one page
    • Personalized Medical Weight LossOur physician-led program with a provider, a registered dietitian and follow-up visits. Medication is not required.
    • GLP-1 Weight LossLabs and a physical first. If a GLP-1 is appropriate for you, medication with nutrition, body-composition tracking and dose management.
    • GLP-1 OptimizationAlready taking a GLP-1 prescribed elsewhere? Your prescriber keeps prescribing; we add a provider review, body-composition tracking and a dietitian visit.
    • Oral Weight-Loss MedicationsNon-injectable options, prescribed after screening as part of a full plan, not on their own.
    • BOUNCE for Children and TeensAges 5 to 18, written for parents. No referral needed; a parent attends every visit.
    • Weight MaintenanceOngoing support and monitoring to help you keep the weight off once you reach your goal.

  • Hormones

    Bloodwork before anything is prescribed, and follow-up labs after. Hormone symptoms and weight are cared for by the same team.

    • Menopause TreatmentHormone therapy for perimenopause and menopause symptoms such as hot flashes, night sweats and sleep disruption, with weight care from the same team.
    • Hormone Therapy (BHRT)Bioidentical hormone replacement therapy for women and men, and where to start.
    • Testosterone Therapy for MenFor men whose bloodwork confirms low testosterone.
