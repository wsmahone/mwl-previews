Aesthetic medicine, in a medical practice

Aesthetic medicine focuses on helping patients look and feel their best through medically guided cosmetic treatments: non-surgical procedures that improve the appearance of the skin, face, and body, performed in a medical setting. Unlike surgical cosmetic procedures, these treatments typically involve minimal downtime and can be tailored to fit your lifestyle and comfort level.

How Jet Plasma works

Jet Plasma technology converts electrical energy into a high-frequency stream of cold plasma. Delivered by a specialized handheld device, the plasma penetrates the skin without damaging the surface layer, triggering cellular repair and regeneration beneath it.

As the skin responds to this stimulation, production of collagen and elastin, two essential components responsible for smooth, firm, and healthy-looking skin, increases, which helps tighten, lift, and improve overall skin quality over time. Because the treatment works beneath the surface, results appear natural and continue to improve as your skin renews itself.

Downtime and what to expect during treatment

Jet Plasma treatments are typically quick and comfortable. A specialized handheld device delivers plasma energy across the treatment area. Most sessions take around 30 minutes, depending on the area being treated.

Because the treatment is non-invasive, patients can usually return to normal activities immediately after their appointment. Some patients experience mild redness or sensitivity that typically resolves within a short period.

Results vary depending on the treatment and the individual. Some improvement can be visible early, while collagen stimulation shows gradual results over several weeks or months, with continued enhancement as collagen production increases after a session. For optimal results, a series of treatments may be recommended; how many, and how far apart, is set at your consultation.

Who is a candidate for Jet Plasma

Jet Plasma is used on many areas of the face and body and on many skin types. People usually ask about it when they want to address fine lines, loose skin, acne or acne scars, uneven tone, enlarged pores, stretch marks, or thinning hair without needles, surgery, or downtime.

Whether it is the right treatment for you is decided at a personalized consultation, where your provider (Dr. Scinta or a nurse practitioner) evaluates your concerns, skin condition, and goals and recommends a customized plan. Some people are better served by a different approach, and some should wait; that is decided at the consultation, not on this page.

Questions people ask before booking

What is Jet Plasma?

Jet Plasma is a non-invasive aesthetic treatment that uses cold plasma technology to stimulate collagen production, improve skin texture, and restore a more youthful appearance, without needles, surgery, or downtime.

A specialized handheld device converts electrical energy into a high-frequency stream of cold plasma that penetrates the skin without damaging the surface layer, activating the body's natural healing process and encouraging the production of collagen and elastin.

Does Jet Plasma hurt?

Jet Plasma treatments are typically quick and comfortable, and there are no needles or injections.

Some patients experience mild redness or sensitivity afterward that typically resolves within a short period. If you have sensitive skin or concerns about comfort, raise them at the consultation so the plan accounts for them.

How long does a session take?

Most sessions take around 30 minutes, depending on the area being treated.

Your consultation confirms the areas to be treated and how long your sessions are likely to run.

Is there any downtime?

Little to no downtime. Because the treatment is non-invasive, patients can usually return to normal activities immediately after their appointment.

Mild redness or sensitivity can occur and typically resolves within a short period.

How many treatments will I need?

For optimal results, a series of treatments may be recommended. The number, and the spacing between sessions, is set at your consultation.

It depends on the concern being treated, the area, and how your skin responds. Results vary from person to person.

When will I see results?

Results vary depending on the treatment and the individual. Some improvement can be visible early, while collagen stimulation shows gradual results over several weeks or months.

Because the treatment works beneath the surface, results appear natural and continue to improve as your skin renews itself and collagen production increases in the weeks after a session.

What areas can be treated?

Jet Plasma treatments are suitable for many areas of the face and body.

Which areas make sense for you is decided at the consultation, based on your concerns and your skin.

Can Jet Plasma help with thinning hair?

Hair thinning or hair loss is among the concerns Jet Plasma can help address, along with fine lines, loose skin, acne scars, uneven tone, enlarged pores, and stretch marks.

Whether it is appropriate for your situation, and what to expect, is discussed at the consultation. Results vary.
