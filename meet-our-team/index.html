Meet Dr. Wendy Scinta and our team

Medical Weight Loss of New York is a physician-led practice in Fayetteville, NY, serving Syracuse and Central New York. Wendy Scinta, MD, MS, founded the practice and directs the medical care, working alongside nurse practitioners, a registered dietitian and the office team.

Founder and medical director

Dr. Wendy Scinta, MD, MS

Dr. Scinta founded Medical Weight Loss of New York and directs the medical care. She treats adult and childhood obesity and is a past president of the Obesity Medicine Association.

She is the author of BOUNCE: A Weight Loss Doctor's Plan for a Happier, Healthier, and Slimmer Child and of the BOUNCE program for children and teens, which the practice offers for ages 5 to 18. At the practice she works alongside nurse practitioners and a registered dietitian; every weight loss plan starts with a medical evaluation and a nutrition visit, and medication is prescribed only when it is appropriate for you.

How we work

What to expect here

Evaluation first, then a plan built from your results. Obesity is treated as a chronic medical condition, with nutrition, physical activity, behavior change and medication when appropriate.

  1. Free Discovery Call

    Up to 30 minutes by phone with our team: how the program works, your questions, and exact pricing before anything is scheduled. Not a medical visit.

  2. Evaluation

    Testing and bloodwork with a nurse, then about 60 minutes with a provider who reviews your bloodwork, history and medications.

  3. Nutrition visit

    About 45 minutes with our registered dietitian to build your daily plan. Weekly group classes in person or on Zoom.

  4. Your plan

    Built from your results. It may involve no medication, an oral medication, a GLP-1, or a combination.

  5. Follow-up and maintenance

    Regular visits to adjust your plan, then a maintenance program once you reach your goal.

Weight loss isn't a mystery, it's a science.

Hormone programs follow the same first rule: bloodwork before anything is prescribed, and follow-up labs after. Results vary and nothing here is a promise. See all programs on one page.

Find Your Form

A tribute to every patient who takes the first step

Find Your Form is our tribute to every patient who has chosen to take a step toward better health. It celebrates the people, past, present and future, who decided to pursue a stronger, healthier version of themselves. Choosing to improve your health takes courage, and we are proud to support patients who are ready to make that commitment.

No two journeys look the same. For some, the moment comes at a weight where it is time to regain control. For others it is a diagnosis such as type 2 diabetes, a new grandchild and the wish to keep up, or the start of a medication for high cholesterol, high blood pressure or sleep apnea.

Whatever brought you here, the important part is that you are ready. To us, Find Your Form is the moment you decide to act on your well-being, and our team is honored to walk alongside you with guidance, encouragement and support. You do not have to do this alone.
