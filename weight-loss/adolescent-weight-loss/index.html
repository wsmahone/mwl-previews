BOUNCE, the program for children and teens ages 5 to 18

BOUNCE is Medical Weight Loss of New York's weight loss program for children and teens ages 5 to 18. The name is an acronym, and Dr. Scinta's book of the same name is based on the program. Like all medical weight loss at the practice, it is a comprehensive approach that focuses on four areas: nutrition, exercise, behavioral modification, and medical management. It begins with a detailed review of health, medical history, bloodwork, and past experiences, because no two patients start from the same place.

What the BOUNCE approach includes

  • A detailed medical review of your child's health, history, and bloodwork
  • Personalized nutrition counseling and meal planning with a registered dietitian trained in pediatric weight management
  • Exercise guidance
  • Ongoing support and accountability at regular visits
  • Plain explanations to you, the parent or guardian, at every step

What the plan includes for your child, and how visits are scheduled, is set by the provider after the evaluation and explained to you first. Nothing is decided on a call or on this page.

How it works

Three steps: a free call with you, a medical evaluation of your child at our Fayetteville office, then follow-up visits with support. You are with your child at every visit, and nothing medical is decided before the evaluation.

  1. Free Discovery Call, for parents

    About 30 minutes with our team. We review how BOUNCE works for children and teens, what a first visit involves, what history or records are helpful, and detailed pricing, and we answer your questions. Nothing medical is decided on this call.

  2. Medical evaluation at our Fayetteville office

    Your child is evaluated by a provider, with you present, including a review of health, medical history, and bloodwork, and you meet with the practice's registered dietitian. The provider explains what is recommended for your child and why before anything starts.

  3. Follow-up visits and support

    Regular visits track progress and adjust the plan, with nutrition guidance and support along the way. A parent or guardian attends every visit. How often you come in is set by the provider and reviewed with you on the call.

What parents can expect

No two families arrive the same way. Some parents call after a checkup raised a concern; others after watching a child struggle with energy, sleep, or confidence. The evaluation exists to find out what is going on and what would help, including when the answer is not a weight program.

Why a medical practice, not a diet

The practice treats weight as a medical matter, with a provider evaluation first and a plan built from your child's own history and bloodwork. The practice's stated mission is to get every patient to a healthy weight for their body type; how much and how fast is set by the provider based on your child's needs, not a number on a website.

Questions parents ask before booking

What ages do you treat?

BOUNCE is for children and teens ages 5 to 18. The practice's youngest patient to date has been 5 years old.

Whether the program is a fit for your child's situation is discussed on the call and confirmed by the provider at the evaluation.

Is this page for my child to read?

No. This page, and the form on it, are written for parents and guardians.

Please do not enter a child's name or details on the form. We contact you, the adult, to set up the call.

What happens on the free Discovery Call?

It is a free call of about 30 minutes with our team, with you as the parent or guardian. We review how BOUNCE works for children and teens, what the first visit involves, answer your questions, and provide detailed pricing.

It is not a medical visit, and nothing medical is decided on it.

Who will see my child?

Your child's evaluation is with Wendy Scinta, MD, MS, or one of her nurse practitioners; all of the practice's providers are trained to see BOUNCE patients. The nutrition visit is with Martha Pascucci, RD, the practice's registered dietitian.

Dr. Scinta is board certified in Family Medicine and a Diplomate of the American Board of Obesity Medicine, is a past president of the Obesity Medicine Association, and is the author of BOUNCE, a book in the field of childhood obesity. Martha holds a certificate in Pediatric Weight Management from the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics.

Do I need a referral from my child's pediatrician?

No. A referral is not needed; you can request the Discovery Call directly.

Records from your pediatrician are still welcome. Recent bloodwork your pediatrician has done can be reviewed, and the call covers what is helpful to bring to the evaluation.

Do parents attend the visits?

Yes. A parent or guardian is with the child for all visits, the evaluation and every follow-up.

You hear every recommendation directly from the provider, and nothing is decided for your child without you in the room.

Is the program the same as the adult program?

BOUNCE is the practice's own program for children and teens ages 5 to 18. The approach covers the same four areas as the adult program: nutrition, exercise, behavioral modification, and medical management. How they apply to a child or teen is decided by the provider for your child.

The practice treats patients across the lifespan, and its registered dietitian is trained in both adult and pediatric weight management. What your child's plan includes is explained to you after the evaluation.

Will my child be prescribed anything?

Nothing medical is decided on the call or on this page. What a plan includes for your child is decided by the provider at the medical evaluation and explained to you first.

Any recommendation is made with you present, based on your child's evaluation, history, and bloodwork.

What does the program cost?

Cost depends on the plan and your child's situation. Detailed pricing is reviewed on the free Discovery Call, before anything is scheduled.

No payment is taken on this website.

Does insurance cover it?

Medical Weight Loss of New York is an out-of-network practice and does not submit charges to insurance plans. Superbills are provided for you to submit to your insurer for reimbursement.

Payment options include cash, check, major credit cards, HSA and FSA cards, CareCredit, and Cherry payment plans.

Are visits in person?

The evaluation is in person at our Fayetteville office. Telemedicine visits are offered, but they may need to be alternated with office visits, depending on the program and the provider's input.

How your child's follow-up visits are scheduled is reviewed on the call.

What should we bring to the first visit?

Any recent bloodwork, a list of current medications, and a note of what you have tried before.

The Discovery Call covers exactly what is helpful for your child's evaluation.

Do you see families from outside Syracuse?

Yes. We are in Fayetteville, on East Genesee Street minutes from I-481, and see patients from Syracuse, Manlius, DeWitt, East Syracuse, and across Central New York, with patients also traveling from elsewhere in Upstate New York and surrounding areas.

The evaluation is in person.

What is BOUNCE?

BOUNCE is the name of Medical Weight Loss of New York's weight loss program for children and teens ages 5 to 18, and of Dr. Scinta's book on childhood obesity, BOUNCE: A Weight Loss Doctor's Plan for a Happier, Healthier, and Slimmer Child, which is based on the program.

The name is an acronym. The program has been adopted by schools, practices, and organizations across the United States and internationally, and it is the program your child would follow here.
