What Is Phentermine?

Phentermine is a prescription appetite-suppressing medication that has been used in medical weight management for decades.

For appropriately selected patients, reducing hunger can make it easier to follow a nutritional plan, control portions, decrease snacking, and create the calorie reduction necessary for weight loss.

More than a pill

But prescribing phentermine is not simply about giving someone a pill. The right dose, timing, medical screening, nutritional strategy, and ongoing monitoring all matter.

Our medical team determines whether phentermine is appropriate for you and monitors your response throughout treatment.

Why Experience With Phentermine Matters

Phentermine has been available for many years, but that does not mean every patient should be treated the same way. At Medical Weight Loss of New York, weight-loss medications are prescribed as part of an individualized treatment plan.

We consider factors such as:

  • How much weight you need to lose
  • Your hunger and appetite patterns
  • Your blood pressure and cardiovascular history
  • Other medications you take
  • Previous weight-loss medications you have tried
  • Medical conditions that may affect treatment
  • Your response to medication over time
  • Your nutrition, activity level, and overall weight-loss strategy
The right treatment, not the highest dose

The goal is not to use the highest dose possible. The goal is to use the right treatment for the right patient and make adjustments as your needs change.

Is Phentermine Right for Me?

Possibly. Phentermine may be considered for certain patients who would benefit from additional help controlling appetite as part of a medically supervised weight-loss program.

But it is not appropriate for everyone. Before prescribing phentermine, our medical team reviews your health history, medications, blood pressure, cardiovascular history, thyroid history, kidney function, and other important risk factors.

Screening comes first

Certain conditions and medication combinations can make phentermine inappropriate, including some cardiovascular diseases, uncontrolled high blood pressure, hyperthyroidism, glaucoma, pregnancy, breastfeeding, and recent use of MAO inhibitor medications. Your provider will complete the appropriate screening and determine whether phentermine is safe and appropriate for you.

What About Other Oral Weight-Loss Medications?

Phentermine is not the only oral medication available for weight management.

Depending on your medical history, previous treatment, eating behaviors, other medications, and individual needs, your provider may recommend a different prescription oral anti-obesity medication.

Different medications target different aspects of weight regulation, hunger, appetite, cravings, and eating behavior. A medication that works extremely well for one person may not be the best choice for another.

Evaluated for you

Our providers evaluate the available options and determine which treatment, or combination of treatment strategies, makes the most sense for you.

What About Oral GLP-1 Medications?

Weight-loss treatment continues to evolve, and GLP-1 therapy is no longer limited exclusively to injections. Wegovy (semaglutide) is now available in an FDA-approved oral tablet form for chronic weight management in appropriate adults.

Because GLP-1 medications work differently from traditional appetite suppressants such as phentermine, we discuss them separately as part of our GLP-1 Weight Loss Program. Your provider can help you understand the differences and determine which type of medication is most appropriate for you.
