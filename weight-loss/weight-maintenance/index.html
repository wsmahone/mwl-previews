Why continue with maintenance

Ongoing support can make a significant difference in long-term success. Our maintenance programs are designed to help you:

  • Reinforce healthy habits
  • Avoid slipping back into old routines
  • Receive continued medical oversight
  • Stay accountable and motivated
  • Support long-term metabolic health

Our team remains by your side so you can stay confident and in control of your health. Our goal is to help you maintain your results and continue building a healthier, stronger lifestyle for years to come.

How maintenance works here

Maintenance at Medical Weight Loss NY is a schedule of visits and classes, set by the plan you choose, with the team that follows your weight over time.

  1. Free Discovery Call, about 20 to 30 minutes

    A conversation with our team, not a medical visit. We review the three maintenance plans, answer your questions, and give you detailed pricing before anything is scheduled.

  2. Choose your plan and your visit rhythm

    Basic Stability is a monthly nurse visit with Tanita Body Composition Analysis and a provider visit every 3 months. Core Maintenance alternates provider and nurse visits each month, with monthly body composition analysis. Elite Metabolic is a monthly provider visit with monthly body composition analysis, priority scheduling, and two specialty lab panels a year.

  3. Classes and ongoing support

    Every plan includes 6 nutrition or group classes per year, in-office or on Zoom. Elite Metabolic adds quarterly small-group nutrition coaching and 10% off supplements and products (excluding GLP-1 medications). Our team remains by your side so you can stay accountable, motivated, and in control of your health.

Questions people ask before booking

What is the Maintenance Program?

A structured program for after you complete your weight loss journey: ongoing support, medical guidance, and accountability so you keep the progress you worked for.

There are three plans with different visit schedules, and every plan includes 6 nutrition or group classes per year, in-office or on Zoom.

Which maintenance plan should I choose?

It depends on how much support you want and how often you want to be seen. Basic Stability is a monthly nurse visit with body composition analysis and a provider visit every 3 months. Core Maintenance, the most popular plan, alternates provider and nurse visits each month. Elite Metabolic is a provider visit every month plus quarterly small-group nutrition coaching, priority scheduling, 10% off supplements and products (excluding GLP-1 medications), and two specialty lab panels a year.

We review all three on the free Discovery Call and help you pick the one that fits your needs, goals, and level of support.

What does the Maintenance Program cost?

Basic Stability is $79 a month, Core Maintenance is $100 a month, and Elite Metabolic is $159 a month, as of October 2026. Pricing is confirmed on your free Discovery Call, before anything is scheduled.

We are an out-of-network practice and do not bill insurance directly. Certain parts of care may be eligible for reimbursement, and we provide a superbill for you to submit. Cash, check, major credit cards, HSA and FSA cards, CareCredit, and Cherry payment plans are accepted.

Are the nutrition classes in person or online?

Both. Every plan includes 6 nutrition or group classes per year, held in-office or on Zoom.

Elite Metabolic adds quarterly small-group nutrition coaching.

How do I start the Maintenance Program?

Request a free Discovery Call with the form on this page or call 315-445-0023. We call you back to set a time, usually within one business day.

The call is 20 to 30 minutes and is not a medical visit. We review the three plans, answer your questions, and give you pricing; if you want to proceed, we schedule your first maintenance visit at our Fayetteville office.

Do you see patients from outside Syracuse?

Yes. We are in Fayetteville, on East Genesee Street minutes from I-481, and see patients from Syracuse, Manlius, DeWitt, East Syracuse, and across Central New York, with patients also traveling from elsewhere in Upstate New York and surrounding areas.

Maintenance visits are in person at our Fayetteville office; the nutrition and group classes are in-office or on Zoom.
