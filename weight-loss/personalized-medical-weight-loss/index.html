Our approach

A comprehensive, science-based approach to weight management

At Medical Weight Loss of New York, we take a comprehensive, science-based approach to weight management. Instead of putting every patient on the same diet or automatically prescribing the same medication, we build your treatment plan around your health, lifestyle, eating patterns, previous weight-loss experiences, and goals.

Whether you want to lose 30 pounds, 50 pounds, 100 pounds or more, our goal is not simply to help you lose weight. We want to help you improve your health and develop a realistic strategy for maintaining your progress long term.

For more than 20 years, our team has helped thousands of patients throughout Syracuse and Central New York who have struggled with their weight, often after trying multiple diets, exercise programs, medications, or weight-loss plans without lasting success.

Why Personalized Weight Loss Matters

There is no single weight-loss plan that works for everyone.

One person may struggle primarily with hunger. Another may be a night eater or frequent snacker. Someone else may have difficulty planning meals, may need more structure, or may have medical conditions or medications that affect their weight.

That is why we start with a clean slate.

We look at the whole person, identify the factors that may be contributing to weight gain or making weight loss more difficult, and then determine which tools make the most sense for you.

Your treatment plan may include nutritional changes, behavioral strategies, exercise guidance, prescription weight-loss medication, meal replacements, or a combination of approaches.

And as your body, health, and needs change, your plan changes with you.

The MWL difference

We don't sell one weight-loss program. We determine what may be preventing you from losing weight and build the treatment around you.

The Four Pillars of Our Weight Loss Program

Our personalized approach is built around four important areas of obesity medicine: nutrition, medical management, behavioral management, and exercise.

Nutrition

There is no perfect diet. The best nutritional plan is one that helps you lose weight, supports your health, and is realistic enough to follow in your everyday life.

You may have done well with a low-carbohydrate or keto-style plan in the past but no longer have the time to shop, cook, and plan every meal. In that situation, a more structured program or the use of meal replacements may make more sense. Other patients may prefer a food-based plan using meals they prepare at home.

Our team works with you to find an approach that fits your lifestyle rather than forcing your lifestyle to fit a particular diet.

Medical Management

For some patients, lifestyle changes alone are not enough. Prescription weight-loss medications can be an important tool when they are medically appropriate.

Depending on your health history, goals, previous treatment, hunger levels, and other factors, your provider may discuss options such as GLP-1 medications, phentermine, or other oral weight-loss medications.

Medication is not the program. It is one tool that may be used as part of a much broader medical weight-loss strategy.

Your provider will help determine whether medication is appropriate for you, which option makes the most sense, and how it should be incorporated into your overall treatment plan. As you lose weight and weight-related medical conditions improve, your healthcare providers may also be able to adjust certain medications when medically appropriate.

Behavioral Management

Knowing what to eat is only part of successful weight management. How, when, and why you eat can be just as important.

Some people struggle with frequent snacking. Others eat most of their calories at night, experience episodes of overeating, eat in response to stress, or have difficulty controlling hunger in certain situations.

These behaviors are not all treated the same way. We work to identify your individual eating patterns and develop strategies specifically for the problems that are getting in your way. Depending on the situation, treatment may involve behavioral strategies, nutritional changes, medication, or a combination of approaches.

Exercise

Exercise is important for overall health and long-term weight management, but more exercise is not always better, especially at the beginning of a weight-loss program.

For some patients, suddenly increasing exercise can substantially increase hunger and make it harder to stay on their nutritional plan.

We help you develop an activity strategy based on your current fitness level, health, hunger response, and stage of weight loss. As you progress and move closer to weight maintenance, exercise can be gradually increased to help improve fitness, support lean mass, preserve your progress, and support long-term health.

Do I Have to Take a GLP-1 to Lose Weight?

No.

GLP-1 medications can be extremely useful for appropriate patients, but they are not the only way to achieve meaningful weight loss.

Some patients may benefit from a GLP-1 medication. Others may do very well with phentermine or another oral medication. Some patients may not need prescription weight-loss medication at all.

At Medical Weight Loss of New York, we do not start with a particular drug and try to fit you into that treatment.

We start with you.

Then we determine which tools are most likely to help you succeed.

How the Program Works

We start with a blank slate. Your plan is designed from your individual results after a complete physical and a nutrition visit, not before them. It begins with a free Discovery Call.

  1. Free Discovery Call

    A free call with our team. We go over how the program works, answer your questions, and review pricing. Nothing medical is decided on this call.

  2. Complete physical, Part 1

    Your first office visit is with a nurse for testing and bloodwork. Recent bloodwork from your own doctor can be reviewed, and EKGs from the past six months are accepted.

  3. Complete physical, Part 2

    A visit of about 60 minutes with a provider, who reviews your bloodwork, medical history, medications, eating patterns, and past experiences with you.

  4. Nutrition visit

    About 45 minutes with our registered dietitian to strategize your daily plan. Meal replacements are optional.

  5. Your customized plan

    Your plan is designed from your individual results. It may involve no medication, a generic medication such as phentermine, a GLP-1 medication, or a combination, considered case by case when it is clinically appropriate.

  6. Follow-up and maintenance

    Regular follow-up visits monitor your progress, identify problems early, and adjust your plan along the way. When you reach your goal, the focus changes from losing weight to maintaining it through our weight maintenance program.

Your Program Evolves as You Lose Weight

A successful weight-loss program should not remain exactly the same from beginning to end. Your hunger can change. Your nutritional needs can change. Your medications may change. Your exercise capacity improves. Strategies that worked during the first few weeks of weight loss may not be the same strategies you need six months later.

That is why ongoing follow-up is such an important part of our approach. We monitor your progress, identify problems early, and adjust your plan along the way to help you continue moving toward your goals.

And when you reach your goal, the focus changes from losing weight to maintaining it. Long-term weight maintenance is not an afterthought. It is part of the plan from the beginning.

More Than 20 Years of Medical Weight Loss Experience

Weight management has changed tremendously over the past two decades. New medications have become available. Our understanding of obesity has improved. Treatment options have expanded.

But one principle has remained the same: there is no single solution that is right for every patient.

With more than 20 years of experience treating patients with overweight and obesity, Medical Weight Loss of New York combines established weight-management strategies with today's medical treatment options to create a plan designed around the individual patient.

Whether you have struggled with weight for several years or for most of your life, we want to understand what you have already tried, what worked, what did not, and what may help you succeed now.
