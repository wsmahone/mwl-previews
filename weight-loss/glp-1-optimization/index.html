Is this program for you?

This program is for patients who already have a GLP-1 prescribed by another healthcare provider or an online company and want medical, nutrition, and body composition support around it. Medical Weight Loss of New York does not prescribe your GLP-1 in this program; your outside provider keeps doing that.

It fits if you

  • Get your GLP-1 from another provider or an online company and are not getting the medical, nutrition, and body composition support that goes with it
  • Do not have a daily meal plan or a protein target
  • Are losing weight, and no one is checking your body composition to see how much of it is lean muscle
  • Have side effects and cannot get answers
  • Have had your dose increased without anyone reviewing how you are actually doing
  • Want a plan for long-term success, not only the next refill

It is not the right fit if you

  • Want Medical Weight Loss of New York to prescribe and manage your GLP-1. That is our GLP-1 Weight Loss Program, a separate program with its own evaluation.
  • Are not currently taking a GLP-1. This program supports a medication you already have. If you are considering one, start with the GLP-1 Weight Loss Program or Personalized Medical Weight Loss.

Questions people ask before booking

Who is the Outside GLP-1 Optimization Program for?

Patients who already receive their GLP-1 medication from another provider, including online companies, and want added medical, nutrition, and body composition support.

Many people receive GLP-1 medication elsewhere but still need help with an appropriate daily meal plan, body composition, protein intake, lean muscle preservation, side effects, and long-term success. This program adds that support from Medical Weight Loss of New York in Fayetteville, NY, while your outside provider continues prescribing the medication. If you are not currently taking a GLP-1, see our GLP-1 Weight Loss Program instead.

Does Medical Weight Loss of New York prescribe my GLP-1 in this program?

No. While you are in the GLP-1 Optimization Program, Medical Weight Loss of New York is providing support and optimization guidance, not acting as your GLP-1 prescriber.

Your outside provider remains responsible for prescribing. If you later want MWL to take over prescribing, there is a separate transition process into our Comprehensive Weight Loss Program, described on this page.

What does the $199 initial assessment include?

A clinical assessment (blood pressure, pulse, pulse oximeter, and Tanita Body Composition Analysis), a 45-minute provider review, and a 30-minute registered dietitian visit.

The provider review is a focused medical review of your current GLP-1 medication and medical history, overall progress, side effects, goals, and muscle-preservation needs. The dietitian visit addresses meal structure, protein intake, reduced appetite, and muscle preservation. The introductory special is $199 and the regular price is $249, as of September 2026.

Is the monthly membership required?

No. The monthly membership is optional.

It is $129 per month, as of September 2026, for ongoing GLP-1 optimization support: body composition monitoring, medication review, nutrition review, and progress support. You can start with the initial assessment and decide about the membership afterward.

How often are visits in the membership?

Provider visits are every 4 weeks and 25 minutes in length.

Each visit reviews your current GLP-1 dose, effectiveness, and tolerance, with Tanita Body Composition Analysis and a review of weight loss and lean muscle preservation. Ongoing nutrition review and progress support are also part of the membership, while your outside provider remains responsible for prescribing.

Can I get more nutrition time?

Yes. Additional nutrition support is available as needed at $26 per 15 minutes, as of September 2026.

The initial assessment includes a 30-minute registered dietitian visit, and the membership includes ongoing nutrition review covering protein intake, meal structure, appetite changes, and practical adjustments. If you want more time with the dietitian, it is available in 15-minute increments.

What if I later want Medical Weight Loss of New York to prescribe my GLP-1?

You would transition into our Comprehensive Weight Loss Program through a separate transition process.

Because your focused provider review, Tanita body composition analysis, and initial registered dietitian visit were already completed, you only need the additional testing and follow-up required for MWL to safely assume management: a nurse visit with bloodwork review or collection as needed, resting metabolic rate (RMR) testing, and an EKG, $271. An acceptable EKG from the last 12 months subtracts $95, and recent bloodwork can be reviewed before the nurse visit. After transfer, provider follow-up is $90 per visit at 2 weeks, $125 at 3 weeks, or $150 at 4 weeks, with the frequency set by your provider. Prices as of September 2026.

Do you take insurance, and how is lab work billed?

Medical Weight Loss of New York is an out-of-network medical practice and does not accept insurance for office visits or program fees. Lab work is coordinated through LabCorp and is billed through your insurance.

Program fees on this page are as of September 2026. We review current pricing with you when we call to schedule.

Should I bring my dose history and labs to the first visit?

Yes. Bring what you have: your current GLP-1 medication and dose history, and any recent bloodwork or EKG.

The 45-minute provider review is a focused review of your current GLP-1 medication and medical history, so your dose history helps. Recent bloodwork and an acceptable EKG from the last 12 months matter if you later transition to MWL prescribing: the EKG subtracts $95 from the transition nurse visit, and we can review your bloodwork first and order only what is still needed. Prices as of September 2026.

How is this different from the GLP-1 Weight Loss Program?

In the Outside GLP-1 Optimization Program another provider prescribes your GLP-1 and Medical Weight Loss of New York provides support and optimization guidance. In the GLP-1 Weight Loss Program, Medical Weight Loss of New York prescribes and manages your GLP-1.

Simple way to think about it: if another provider is prescribing your GLP-1, the right fit is the Outside GLP-1 Optimization Program. If Medical Weight Loss of New York is prescribing and managing your GLP-1, you are in the Comprehensive Weight Loss Program, the program described on our GLP-1 Weight Loss Program page.
