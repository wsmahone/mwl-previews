Why men get evaluated

Low testosterone is diagnosed with bloodwork, not from symptoms. But men usually decide to get checked after noticing changes like these, especially when several show up together and stick around:

  • Lower energy, or fatigue that rest does not fix
  • Declining strength or muscle despite training
  • Slower recovery from workouts
  • Reduced libido
  • Poor sleep
  • Trouble concentrating, or less drive than you used to have
  • Changes in body composition

These changes have many possible causes, and testosterone is only one of them. The evaluation exists to find out what is actually going on for you, including when the answer is not hormones.

How it works: treatment that starts with data

Treatment here begins with bloodwork and a medical review. Whether to treat, and at what dose, is decided from your labs and history, not from symptoms alone and not from a standard dose.

  1. Free Discovery Call

    An intro call of up to 30 minutes with our team. Ask anything, learn what labs and history are needed, and hear what the evaluation costs. Nothing medical is decided on this call.

  2. Comprehensive bloodwork and a 40-minute review

    An extensive blood panel, not a single testosterone number, followed by a provider visit of roughly 40 minutes to go through your results and history with you. Recent labs from your own doctor are welcome.

  3. An individualized plan, started conservatively

    If treatment is appropriate, your provider sets a dosing range for you specifically, based on your labs, weight, and health profile, and begins at the conservative end of it where clinically appropriate.

  4. Repeat labs, typically 4 to 6 weeks in

    Key bloodwork is rechecked after treatment begins to see how you are responding.

  5. Adjust to your response

    Your provider reviews the results with you. If a booster is medically appropriate, your provider explains it and its cost before anything is done, and your dosage is reviewed again at the next procedure.

  6. Ongoing care

    Men on pellet therapy here have historically repeated treatment about every six months, with the lab monitoring your provider considers appropriate over time. Your schedule is individualized.

Why men consider pellet therapy

Pellet therapy is the approach many of our male patients choose, mostly for practical reasons:

  • Placed periodically rather than applied every day
  • Individualized by your provider, not a set dose
  • Less frequent intervention than many other delivery methods
  • Followed with lab monitoring like every approach we offer
  • Stay in the bloodstream longer and maintain higher levels than other methods

Pellets are not the right option for everyone. Your provider will tell you what monitoring they require and, plainly, if another approach fits you better.

Questions men ask before booking

What is testosterone replacement therapy?

Testosterone replacement therapy (TRT) is medical treatment for men whose testosterone is low on bloodwork and who have related symptoms.

It is prescribed and monitored by a clinician. The preferred delivery method at Medical Weight Loss of NY is pellets, because they reach higher levels and last longer than injections or creams. The decision to treat is made after labs and a medical review, never on symptoms alone.

What does BHRT mean for men?

For men, "bioidentical" hormone therapy usually means testosterone delivered as pellets or injections.

Bioidentical means identical to what the body makes, which makes it different from other (synthetic) forms. The pellets are plant-based and made from organic yams. Think of it as physician-managed testosterone therapy with dosing determined from your labs.

How do I know if I have low testosterone?

Only bloodwork interpreted by a clinician can tell you. Symptoms are a reason to get evaluated, not a diagnosis.

Fatigue, low libido, or slower recovery have many possible causes. The evaluation exists to find out whether hormones are part of the picture for you and to rule out other explanations.

What bloodwork is required before treatment?

A comprehensive blood panel, reviewed with you by a provider before any treatment decision.

Your provider will tell you exactly which labs are included for your situation and why. Ongoing care includes the follow-up monitoring your provider considers appropriate, including blood counts and other related labs where indicated.

Can I bring recent labs from my primary care doctor?

Yes, please do.

Recent results are useful, and your provider will tell you whether anything needs to be repeated or added before a decision can be made.

What if my labs come back normal?

Then testosterone therapy is not recommended, and we tell you that plainly.

You pay for the evaluation only, and you leave knowing what your labs show and what else might explain how you feel.

What does the pellet procedure involve?

A brief in-office procedure: after numbing the area with local anesthetic, your provider places small pellets under the skin, usually in the upper buttocks region, through a tiny incision.

The visit itself takes minutes. Your provider will explain aftercare, which typically includes avoiding heavy exercise for a few days.

How long do testosterone pellets last?

Men at our practice have typically repeated pellet treatment approximately every six months.

The interval is individualized. Your provider sets your schedule based on your labs and how you respond.

How much does men's pellet therapy cost?

Pellet treatment for men is $750 per treatment as of September 2026, typically about every six months, which works out to about $125 per month.

The initial medical consultation is $225 and the follow-up visit at about six weeks is $100, each charged once, the first time only. Lab work is billed separately and quoted exactly on your Discovery Call. The Discovery Call is free.

What happens at the follow-up, typically 4 to 6 weeks in?

Key labs are repeated and your provider reviews your response.

If clinically appropriate, your plan is adjusted, which may include considering a booster, with its cost explained first. Your dosage is reviewed again at your next procedure.

Are testosterone pellets better than injections?

Medical Weight Loss NY prefers pellets for multiple reasons.

Pellets are placed periodically and avoid daily or weekly dosing; injections follow a different schedule. Pellets reach higher levels and last longer than injections. Testosterone levels remain consistent in the bloodstream for the six-month interval and do not rise and fall like injections do.

Does Medical Weight Loss NY offer testosterone injections?

Yes. Injections are offered as an alternative, or as a trial before moving to pellets, but pellets are the preferred method of hormone therapy.

Can testosterone therapy help with energy or libido?

Yes, testosterone therapy can help with energy and libido.

For men with confirmed low testosterone, treatment is intended to bring levels into an appropriate range, and many men report improvement in how they feel. Results vary from person to person.

Testosterone products are approved for low testosterone caused by specific medical conditions; whether treatment helps age-related declines is still studied, and symptoms alone do not mean testosterone is the cause. That is why the evaluation comes first.

Can I start treatment based on symptoms alone?

No. Treatment decisions require bloodwork and a medical review.

Who should not receive testosterone therapy?

Commonly, men with a history of prostate or breast cancer, men planning to have children, and men with certain blood, heart, or sleep conditions need a different plan or should not be treated.

Your provider decides based on your history, examination, and labs, and will tell you plainly if therapy is not appropriate for you.

Will testosterone therapy affect fertility?

It can. Testosterone therapy may reduce sperm production, which matters if you are planning to have children.

Tell your provider if fertility is a consideration; it changes the conversation about which approach, if any, is appropriate.

Does insurance cover BHRT?

Pellet therapy is generally self-pay at our practice. Some lab work may be covered depending on your plan.

Our team will walk through costs and what to ask your insurer during the Discovery Call.

Do you treat men outside Syracuse?

Yes. We are in Fayetteville and see men from across Central New York, with patients also traveling from elsewhere in Upstate New York and surrounding areas.

What is the difference between the free Discovery Call and a medical consultation?

The Discovery Call is a free intro call of up to 30 minutes with our team about how the process works. It is not a medical visit.

The medical evaluation, with lab work and a provider visit, is the next step if you choose to proceed, and it has its own cost.

Can my wife or partner join the Discovery Call?

Yes. Anyone you choose is welcome on the call, and many men bring the person who encouraged them to look into it.
