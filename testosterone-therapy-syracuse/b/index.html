Why men here choose pellet therapy

Pellets are the method most of our male patients choose, and the one Medical Weight Loss NY prefers. The reasons are practical:

  • They stay in the bloodstream longer and maintain higher levels than other methods
  • Levels stay consistent across the six-month interval instead of rising and falling the way they do with injections
  • Placed about every six months, so there is no daily cream or weekly shot to keep up with
  • Your dose is determined from your labs, weight, and health profile, then rechecked with follow-up bloodwork
  • Plant-based and made from organic yams; bioidentical means identical to what your body makes

Not the right fit for everyone

Your provider will tell you plainly if another approach fits you better. Injections are available as an alternative, or as a trial before moving to pellets, and men with certain histories or plans to have children need a different conversation. Every plan is followed with lab monitoring.

How it works

Three steps, and the first one is free. Whether to treat, and at what dose, is decided from your labs and history, never from symptoms alone.

  1. Free Discovery Call

    Up to 30 minutes with our team. Ask anything, and hear what the evaluation involves and what it costs. Nothing medical is decided on this call.

  2. Comprehensive labs and a 40-minute review

    An extensive blood panel, then a provider visit of roughly 40 minutes to go through your results and history. Recent labs from your own doctor are welcome.

  3. Your plan, then follow-up labs

    If treatment is appropriate, your provider sets your dose from your results and starts conservatively. Key labs are rechecked about 4 to 6 weeks in, and your plan is adjusted to how you respond.

Questions men ask before booking

How much does men's pellet therapy cost?

Pellet treatment for men is $750 per treatment as of September 2026, typically about every six months, which works out to about $125 per month.

The initial medical consultation is $225 and the follow-up visit at about six weeks is $100, each charged once, the first time only. Lab work is billed separately and quoted exactly on your Discovery Call. The Discovery Call is free.

How long do testosterone pellets last?

Men at our practice have typically repeated pellet treatment approximately every six months.

The interval is individualized. Your provider sets your schedule based on your labs and how you respond.

What does the pellet procedure involve?

A brief in-office procedure: after numbing the area with local anesthetic, your provider places small pellets under the skin, usually in the upper buttocks region, through a tiny incision.

The visit itself takes minutes. Your provider will explain aftercare, which typically includes avoiding heavy exercise for a few days.

What bloodwork is required before treatment?

A comprehensive blood panel, reviewed with you by a provider before any treatment decision.

Your provider will tell you exactly which labs are included for your situation and why. Ongoing care includes the follow-up monitoring your provider considers appropriate, including blood counts and other related labs where indicated.

What if my labs come back normal?

Then testosterone therapy is not recommended, and we tell you that plainly.

You pay for the evaluation only, and you leave knowing what your labs show and what else might explain how you feel.

Does Medical Weight Loss NY offer testosterone injections?

Yes. Injections are offered as an alternative, or as a trial before moving to pellets, but pellets are the preferred method of hormone therapy.

Does insurance cover BHRT?

Pellet therapy is generally self-pay at our practice. Some lab work may be covered depending on your plan.

Our team will walk through costs and what to ask your insurer during the Discovery Call.
